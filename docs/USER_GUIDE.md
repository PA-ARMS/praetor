# User Guide for Attorneys and Paralegals

## Welcome to Virginia Legal AI

This system helps you research Virginia and Federal law, generate legal forms, and analyze case outcomes using AI-powered legal research.

## Getting Started

### 1. Logging In

1. Open your web browser
2. Navigate to the system URL (provided by your IT administrator)
3. Enter your username and password
4. Click "Login"

**Note:** If you forget your password, contact your system administrator.

### 2. Understanding the Interface

The main interface has three sections:

- **Chat Interface**: Ask legal questions and get AI-powered answers
- **Chat History**: View past conversations (if you have permission)
- **Settings**: Adjust voice and display preferences

## Using Legal Research

### Asking Questions

The AI can answer questions about:
- Virginia statutes and regulations
- Federal law
- Case law analysis
- Legal procedures
- Form requirements

**Example Questions:**
- "What is the statute of limitations for breach of contract in Virginia?"
- "What are the elements of a negligence claim?"
- "How do I file a motion for summary judgment in Fairfax County?"

### Understanding AI Responses

Each response includes:

1. **Answer**: The AI's explanation of the law
2. **Citations**: Legal sources in Bluebook format
3. **Confidence Indicators**: 
   - High confidence (70%+): Strong supporting authority found
   - Medium confidence (50-70%): Some authority found, verify
   - Low confidence (<50%): Limited authority, requires verification
4. **Warnings**: Important notices about:
   - Missing authoritative sources
   - Conflicting authorities
   - Need for attorney review

### Best Practices for Questions

**✅ DO:**
- Ask specific questions: "What is the SOL for personal injury in Virginia?"
- Request citations: "What statute governs this?"
- Ask follow-up questions for clarification
- Verify important answers with primary sources

**❌ DON'T:**
- Rely solely on AI answers for critical decisions
- Use AI responses without attorney review for client matters
- Assume AI has access to all current law (may be outdated)
- Skip verification of citations

## Understanding Confidence Scores

The AI provides confidence scores to help you assess reliability:

- **90-100%**: Very high confidence - multiple authoritative sources found
- **70-89%**: High confidence - authoritative sources found
- **50-69%**: Medium confidence - some sources found, verify
- **Below 50%**: Low confidence - limited sources, requires verification

**Always verify low-confidence answers with primary sources.**

## Warnings and Disclaimers

### When You See Warnings

The system may display warnings when:
- No controlling authority is found
- Only secondary sources are available
- Conflicting authorities exist
- The answer requires attorney judgment

**Take warnings seriously** - they indicate areas requiring additional research or attorney review.

### AI Limitations

This system:
- ✅ Provides research assistance and citations
- ✅ Helps identify relevant law
- ✅ Generates form templates
- ❌ Does NOT replace attorney judgment
- ❌ Does NOT provide legal advice
- ❌ May not have the most current law
- ❌ Cannot access case-specific facts you haven't provided

## Using Form Generation

### Generating Forms

1. Click "Forms" in the interface
2. Select form type (e.g., "Motion for Summary Judgment")
3. Enter case information
4. Review generated form
5. **Always review and edit before filing**

### Form Workflow

The system provides:
- Form templates with placeholders
- Filing instructions
- Required attachments checklist
- Court-specific requirements

**Remember:** Generated forms are templates - always customize for your specific case.

## Chat History

### Viewing Your History

- Click "Chat History" tab
- Select a conversation to view
- See all questions and answers
- Export conversations if needed

### Permissions

- **Paralegals**: Can view only their own chats
- **Attorneys**: Can view their own + their paralegals' chats
- **Partners/Admin**: Can view all chats

## Tips for Better Results

### 1. Be Specific

**Better:** "What is the statute of limitations for medical malpractice in Virginia?"
**Worse:** "Tell me about medical malpractice"

### 2. Ask Follow-ups

If the first answer isn't clear, ask:
- "Can you provide more detail on [specific point]?"
- "What are the exceptions to this rule?"
- "What cases have interpreted this statute?"

### 3. Request Citations

Always ask for citations:
- "What statute governs this?"
- "Can you cite the case law on this?"
- "What is the primary authority?"

### 4. Verify Important Answers

For critical matters:
1. Check the cited sources directly
2. Verify statutes are current
3. Review case law for recent developments
4. Consult with supervising attorney

## Common Use Cases

### Researching Statutes

**Question:** "What does Virginia Code § 8.01-243 say about personal injury SOL?"

**What to expect:**
- Direct quote or summary of the statute
- Citation in Bluebook format
- Related sections if relevant
- High confidence if found in corpus

### Analyzing Case Law

**Question:** "How have Virginia courts interpreted the SOL for contract claims?"

**What to expect:**
- Summary of relevant cases
- Key holdings
- Citations to cases
- Medium to high confidence depending on corpus

### Form Generation

**Request:** "Generate a motion for summary judgment for Fairfax Circuit Court"

**What to expect:**
- Form template with placeholders
- Filing instructions
- Required attachments
- Court-specific formatting

## Troubleshooting

### "No controlling authority found"

**What it means:** The system couldn't find authoritative sources in its database.

**What to do:**
- Try rephrasing your question
- Search for specific statute numbers
- Use traditional research methods
- Contact IT if corpus needs updating

### Low Confidence Score

**What it means:** Limited sources found or conflicting information.

**What to do:**
- Verify with primary sources
- Consult with supervising attorney
- Use traditional research to supplement
- Don't rely solely on AI answer

### System Not Responding

**What to do:**
1. Wait 30 seconds (AI may be processing)
2. Refresh the page
3. Check your internet connection
4. Contact IT support if problem persists
