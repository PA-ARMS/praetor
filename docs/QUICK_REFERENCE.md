## Understanding Responses

### Confidence Scores
- **🟢 70-100%**: High confidence - verify citations
- **🟡 50-69%**: Medium - use with caution
- **🔴 <50%**: Low - requires verification

### Warnings
- ⚠️ **No controlling authority**: Use traditional research
- ⚠️ **Conflicting sources**: Review both sides
- ⚠️ **Secondary sources only**: Find primary authority
